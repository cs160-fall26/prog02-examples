2. Reading level (Flesch–Kincaid)

Reads the chosen measure's legal text from data/datapack.json and computes its Flesch–Kincaid grade level. No AI is used: the code just counts words, sentences and syllables.

Ballot measure:

The formula

grade = 0.39 × (words / sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables / words) − 15.59
